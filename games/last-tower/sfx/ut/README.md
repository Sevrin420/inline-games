# sfx/ut: Unreal Tournament (UT99) announcer (Zombie Farm v21)

**Unreal Tournament announcer audio © Epic Games, used under the site owner's parody license.**

Used for: First Blood (the first kill of a run) and the no-hit kill-streak tiers: 25 Killing Spree, 50 Rampage, 100 Dominating,
200 Unstoppable, 300 Godlike. The callouts share one 13 s cooldown with the Halo announcer (`../halo/`).
The source is the original UT99 22 kHz announcer wavs, mirrored in the mismith/ut99announcer soundboard repo. Each clip was trimmed,
loudness normalized (-15 LUFS), converted to mono 44.1 kHz 96 kbps mp3, and verified by speech recognition.
Skipped: Holy Shit (profanity), Headshot (no sensible trigger), Flawless Victory and Wicked Sick (not in the UT99 set), and the
multi-kills (Halo covers those).
